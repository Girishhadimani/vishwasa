Official Guidance

Give the Gift of Life

One organ donor can save up to 8 lives and enhance 50+ lives. Vishwasa promotes organ donor education in North Karnataka while guiding citizens to complete their official legal pledge on NOTTO.

📌 Note: As mandated by Indian organ transplantation regulations, online pledging provides an official card, while family/next-of-kin consent remains essential.
NOTTO Portal (Govt of India) ↗

Organ Donation Myths vs Facts

❌ Myth: Wealthy people get priority on transplant lists.
✅ Fact: Organ allocation is governed strictly by medical urgency & NOTTO waiting list matching.
❌ Myth: Organ donation disfigures the body.
✅ Fact: Donated organs are removed surgically with utmost respect and dignity.
